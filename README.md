HR Analytics Dashboard
Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI solution developed to analyze workforce performance, employee demographics, and attrition trends. The dashboard enables HR teams and business stakeholders to monitor key workforce KPIs, identify attrition patterns, and support data-driven decision-making.

Business Problem

Organizations generate large volumes of employee data, making it difficult to identify workforce trends through spreadsheets alone. HR managers require interactive dashboards to monitor employee distribution, departmental performance, and attrition rates in order to improve retention strategies and workforce planning.

This project transforms raw HR data into meaningful business insights using Power BI.

Business Insights
Key findings from the dashboard include:

Workforce consists of 1,470 employees.
Overall employee attrition is 237 employees (16.12%).
Research & Development has the highest employee count.
Sales Executive is one of the largest job roles in the organization.
Male employees slightly outnumber female employees.
The dashboard enables HR teams to identify departments with higher attrition and supports workforce planning through interactive filtering.
